## Decided
Brand name stays Panindo (from Filipino "paninda", goods for sale). Screened 2026-10-12 (low risk, panindo.app free) and re-confirmed by the user 2026-10-28 after a second naming round. Used in docs/PRD-mobile-app-launch.md, docs/PRD-mobile-readiness.md and site/index.html.

## Tried and ruled out
- Sapat ("enough"), Kahon ("box"), Kanto (corner): user preferred Panindo; Kanto clashes with a karaoke app and a Pokémon region.
- Tinda / TindaStock: "Tinda POS" is a live PH point-of-sale + inventory app (updated Oct 2026); in the US App Store "tinda" returns Tinder and dating apps.
- Suki, Tallyo, Bodega, Kwenta, Lista: existing apps or marks (Suki small-business tracker, Tallyo Shopify stocktake, Kwenta crypto).
- English names: CornerStock was the only clean one (no app, cornerstock.app free, .com for sale) but the user picked Panindo over it. OnHand, NeverOut, Countertop, Wellstocked, TallyUp, Lowdown, Shelf Life, Brim, Topup have live apps or marks; Aplenty clashes with Amazon's food brand; Counterpart with health-SaaS trademarks.

## Watch out
panindo.app was still unregistered on 2026-10-28; the .com is held (blank page since 2001). No formal USPTO or IPOPHL search has been done.
