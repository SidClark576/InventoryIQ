## Decided
Panindo's mark is concept B "Stocked" (docs/brand/b-symbol.svg, lockup docs/brand/b-lockup.svg): a capital P made of nine rounded squares on a 3x4 grid, the empty cell is the P's counter. It replaces the InventoryIQ brand shown in frontend/src/app/AppLayout.tsx and frontend/index.html. Wordmark is geometric lowercase "panindo" with the i's dot drawn as a tick (docs/brand/c-wordmark.svg). Palette from site/styles.css: green #1d6b48, tag yellow #f4c430 (one box can be the accent), ink #1b2420, kraft #eadbc0.

## Tried and ruled out
- A "Shop Window" (P whose counter is a square store window): clearest at 16px but reads as a generic heavy P.
- C "Ticked" (lowercase p with tick): the tick reads as an accent mark and is weakest at small sizes.

## Watch out
At 16px the boxes merge; the favicon needs a simplified small-size cut. No trademark search has been done yet.
