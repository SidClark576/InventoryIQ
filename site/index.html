A notebook can't warn you.

Counts get scribbled on paper and typed in later, or never. You find out an item is gone when a customer asks for it. And low-stock emails sit unread through the morning rush.

Panindo keeps the count on the phone already in your pocket, and buzzes you while there's still time to reorder.

Scan. Adjust. Get warned.

  1. Scan

    Point the camera at any barcode. Known products fill in their own name.

  2. Adjust

    Big + and − buttons. Three taps from scan to saved, with a buzz to confirm.

  3. Get warned

    Set a low-stock level for each item. Your phone gets a push alert when stock drops below it.

A delivery comes in.

Tap Scan, scan the box, enter how many, save. The scanner reopens for the next box, so the whole delivery is in before the paperwork is signed.

No signal in the stockroom? Keep counting.

Counts and adjustments save on the phone and sync the next time the app is open and online. If someone changed an item while you were counting, Panindo asks which number is right. It never overwrites a count silently.

Questions, answered.

Something else? Email support@panindo.app.

Who owns my inventory data?

You do. Owners and Admins can export everything to CSV at any time, on every plan, including Free.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Your last-synced stock list stays on the phone, and stock adjustments and counts save offline. They sync when the app is open and back online.

Adding, editing, or deleting items still needs a connection for now.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Your store moves to the Free plan. If it has more items or members than Free allows, it becomes read-only for new items and new seats until you're under the limit or upgrade again.

You can still see everything, scan, and adjust stock.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones running Android 7 or later. Tablets work, but the layout isn't optimized for them yet.

Phones without Google services, such as recent Huawei models, can scan but won't get push alerts.

Which countries is it for?

The United States and the Philippines, with prices in dollars or pesos. The app is in English.

Is there a web version?

Yes. The web app uses the same account and store as your phone. CSV import lives on the web. Plans can only be bought in the mobile app.

When can I download it?

Panindo isn't on Google Play yet. Join the beta by email and we'll tell you the day it's live.
